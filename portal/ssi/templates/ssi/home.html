How the demo runs

  1. A prospective student clicks Send a request to issue a credential — no login, no details yet, just a fresh connection invitation.
  2. They scan the QR with their wallet to connect, then enter their name, student ID, department and email.
  3. The portal checks those details against the university's enrollment registry — students and faculty alike. No human reviews it; a records match is the gate. A match sends a Student ID or Faculty ID credential offer immediately, whichever the record says they are.
  4. The holder accepts it in their wallet, then opens Login, scans the proof-request QR, and presents the credential.
  5. ACA-Py verifies the presentation against the credential definition above; the portal then creates a session.
  6. They browse Dashboard and Profile with no re-authentication, then log out. What those pages show depends on the role inside the credential.

Same process for everyone — there's no separate faculty-only way to issue a credential. Whoever the registry says you are is what you get.
