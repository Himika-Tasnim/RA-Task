How the demo runs

  1. Registrar issues a Student ID or Faculty ID credential — the holder scans the QR with their wallet and accepts it.
  2. The holder opens Login, scans the proof-request QR, and presents the credential.
  3. ACA-Py verifies the presentation against the credential definition above; the portal then creates a session.
  4. They browse Dashboard and Profile with no re-authentication, then log out. What those pages show depends on the role inside the credential.
