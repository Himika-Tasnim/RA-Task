How this works

  1. Press Connect — this sends them an invitation, it does not connect anything yet.
  2. They open their own Messages page and scan the QR shown there with their wallet. Their wallet asks them to confirm the connection before it sends anything — that's the actual consent step, not a button on this website.
  3. Once their wallet confirms, a real DIDComm connection exists and messages can flow both ways. Either side can end it later from the conversation.
