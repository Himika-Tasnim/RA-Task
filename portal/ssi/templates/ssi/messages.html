How this works

  1. Press Connect and scan the invitation QR with your wallet — that sends a real DID Exchange connection request.
  2. The other person sees Review request on their own Messages page and chooses to accept or reject it right there.
  3. If they accept, they get their own QR and scan it with their own wallet — a second, independent connection, separate from yours.
  4. Only once both connections exist can messages flow, straight to whichever phone they're addressed to — either side can decline instead, and nothing is connected.
